      Shadow vs clipping — the point of this page

      A real engine clips box-shadow exactly like egui.cr clips Painter#box_shadow to the clip rect. What to look for in each case:

      A · overflow: hidden square

      card

      The outset shadow is cut flush at the panel's bottom and right edges — the part outside the panel simply does not paint.

      B · overflow: hidden + border-radius

      card

      The clip is rounded: the shadow is carved by the 24px radius, not by the box — same as a rounded painter clip.

      C · popup inside a clipped panel

          Top: the absolute-positioned menu cannot escape an ancestor overflow clip — menu and shadow are cut. Bottom: a position:fixed menu escapes it — egui.cr popups do this by default (Foreground layer).
